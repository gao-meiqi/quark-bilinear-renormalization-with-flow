
# Why Lattice People Love It

Today, quantities such as $t_0$ and $w_0$ derived from the gradient flow are routinely used for scale setting in lattice QCD simulations.

Historically, the first major success of the gradient flow was not the SFTX itself, but the observation that flowed observables such as \(t^2\langle E(t)\rangle\) are finite and numerically clean. This success motivated the later development of flowed composite operators, ringed fermions, and the small flow-time expansion.
