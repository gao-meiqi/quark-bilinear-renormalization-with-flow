# Quark Bilinear Operators

Quark bilinear operators constitute one of the most important classes of composite operators in QCD.

Examples include

$$
\bar\psi\psi,
\qquad
\bar\psi\gamma_\mu\psi,
\qquad
\bar\psi\gamma_5\psi,
\qquad
\bar\psi\gamma_\mu\gamma_5\psi,
\qquad
\bar\psi\sigma_{\mu\nu}\psi.
$$

The gradient flow offers a convenient framework for constructing renormalized versions of these operators through flowed fermion fields and the small flow-time expansion.

This note focuses on the treatment of quark bilinears within the gradient-flow formalism and prepares the groundwork for matching to continuum renormalization schemes.
